# Dealer AI Plugins — Product Marketing Context

## Product

Dealer AI Plugins is a public collection of 29 open-source, skills-only plugins for ChatGPT and Codex. Dealer Growth Hackers publishes the collection for automotive dealerships, dealer groups, and agencies serving dealers.

The free plugins must remain complete, useful, inspectable, and MIT licensed. Paid offerings are separate implementation resources and professional services; payment does not unlock a deliberately degraded version of a public plugin.

## Audiences

1. **Individual dealerships** — dealer principals, GMs, marketing directors, BDC leaders, and fixed-operations leaders who need repeatable dealership AI workflows.
2. **Dealer groups** — centralized marketing, operations, analytics, and governance teams that need repeatable deployment across rooftops.
3. **Agencies and consultants** — teams that need reusable, client-facing implementation systems and training.

## Value

- Free plugins turn dealership-specific operating knowledge into repeatable ChatGPT and Codex workflows.
- Paid implementation programs reduce the work required to operationalize those workflows across a store, group, or client portfolio.
- Done-for-you audits provide an evidence-backed baseline and prioritized implementation plan.

## Business model

- **Free:** all public plugin packages, source, updates, and public documentation.
- **Annual implementation programs:** proprietary implementation resources, rollout materials, live enablement, and priority support.
- **Professional services:** fixed-scope readiness audits and custom implementation work under separate written terms.
- **Future authenticated MCP services:** existing paid customers may sign in to use entitled features. These services must follow the OpenAI plugin commerce rules in force at launch.

## Go-to-market

Hybrid motion:

- Self-serve discovery through OpenAI, search, tutorials, and the public plugin catalog.
- Sales-assisted founding memberships for dealers, groups, and agencies.
- Sales-led professional services for audits and custom implementations.

## Pricing assumptions to validate

- Dealer: $795/year per rooftop.
- Dealer group: $2,995/year for up to five rooftops.
- Agency: $5,995/year for up to ten active client rooftops.
- Done-for-you readiness audit: starting at $2,500.

These are founding-price hypotheses, not validated willingness-to-pay results. Validate with customer interviews and a Van Westendorp study before enabling self-serve checkout.

## Compliance boundary

OpenAI plugin metadata and packaged skills must not advertise prices, subscriptions, discounts, trials, or upgrades. They may identify the publisher and link to ordinary informational or support pages when relevant. Digital products and services are sold independently on DealerAIPlugins.com; the plugins do not initiate or promote those transactions.

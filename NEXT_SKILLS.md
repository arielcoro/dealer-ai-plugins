# Recommended Next Dealer AI Plugins

Updated October 1, 2026 after reviewing the current 23-plugin catalog. Priorities favor clear dealership jobs, measurable outputs, reusable workflows, and gaps not already covered by the existing library.

## Built October 1, 2026

- Dealer Agent Governance
- Dealer Plugin Router
- Dots Assistant version one
- Dealer AI Shopping Readiness Audit
- Dealer Lead Response Auditor
- Muse Meta Assistant version one

Dots and Muse were built without an authoritative prior instruction packet. Their version-one contracts require capability verification and should be reconciled when verified source instructions become available.

## Build next

1. **Dealer Lead Response Auditor** — Score speed-to-lead, contact cadence, channel coverage, response quality, appointment setting, and handoff leakage. This closes the largest gap between lead generation and sold outcomes.
2. **Dealer Service Retention Designer** — Build declined-service, lapsed-customer, first-service, post-warranty, seasonal, and recall communication programs with measurable retention goals.
3. **Dealer Inventory Aging Strategist** — Segment 30/60/90+ day inventory, diagnose pricing and merchandising causes, and produce vehicle-level action plans.
4. **Dealer BDC Playbook** — Create inbound, outbound, missed-call, internet-lead, objection-handling, no-show, and dead-lead revival scripts with QA scorecards.
5. **Dealer Competitor Intelligence** — Build evidence-backed competitor rooftop profiles used by AI visibility, comparison pages, sales enablement, paid media, and positioning.

## Second wave

6. **Dealer Data Hygiene Audit** — Audit CRM/DMS duplicates, source normalization, consent status, lifecycle stages, lost reasons, and reporting readiness.
7. **Dealer Marketing Vendor Auditor** — Review contracts, fees, attribution claims, overlapping capabilities, data ownership, cancellation terms, and measurable outcomes without recommending a preferred vendor.
8. **Dealer Lead Source Profitability** — Reconcile spend, leads, appointments, shows, sold units, front/back gross, and attribution limitations by source.
9. **Dealer Review Response & Escalation** — Draft brand-consistent responses and route legal, safety, discrimination, employee, and privacy allegations for human review.
10. **Dealer Fixed Ops Capacity Planner** — Translate technician capacity, appointment demand, no-shows, parts constraints, hours per repair order, and effective labor rate into operating actions.
11. **Dealer Paid Media Waste Audit** — Find geographic leakage, brand-term waste, duplicate conversion actions, poor inventory alignment, and campaign-to-CRM measurement gaps across Google and Meta.
12. **Dealer Offer & Disclosure Reviewer** — Review customer-facing offers for clear terms, evidence requirements, expiration dates, eligibility language, and mandatory escalation to qualified legal or compliance staff.

## Strategic additions

13. **Dealer AI Vendor RFP Builder** — Convert a dealership use case into requirements, security questions, data-flow documentation, pilot design, evaluation criteria, and a weighted scorecard.
14. **Dealer Sales Coaching Scorecard** — Turn call transcripts and CRM notes into observable coaching categories without inferring protected traits or employee intent.
15. **Dealer Website Change Request Builder** — Convert audit findings into vendor-ready tickets with acceptance criteria, evidence, priority, and retest steps.
16. **Dealer Accessibility Audit** — Review dealership templates and key journeys against current WCAG practices, clearly separating automated findings from issues requiring expert manual testing.
17. **Dealer Group Governance Kit** — Define permissions, naming conventions, data ownership, AI-use policy, audit cadence, and rooftop-versus-group responsibilities.
18. **Dealer Executive Weekly Brief** — Turn CRM, media, call, inventory, fixed-ops, review, and AI-visibility inputs into a concise, sourced leadership brief with anomalies and assigned next actions.

## Assistant-specific plugins requested

### Dots

Build after receiving the current authoritative instruction packet:

- Purpose and top user jobs
- Existing system instructions
- Available tools and connected data
- Tone and response style
- Safety, privacy, and approval boundaries
- Five representative successful conversations
- Five requests it should not handle

### Muse

Build after receiving the same authoritative materials for the Meta assistant. Do not infer behavior from the assistant name or public product descriptions; package the actual internal workflow and constraints.

## Recommended build order

Start with Lead Response Auditor, Service Retention Designer, Inventory Aging Strategist, BDC Playbook, and Competitor Intelligence. Together they extend the catalog from marketing diagnostics into the dealership's core revenue loop: lead handling, appointment conversion, inventory movement, service retention, and competitive execution.

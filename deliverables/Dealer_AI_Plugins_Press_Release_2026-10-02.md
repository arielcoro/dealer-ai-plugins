## Dealer Growth Hackers Launches 29 Open-Source ChatGPT and Codex Plugins for Automotive Dealerships

### Dealer AI Plugins gives sales, BDC, marketing, fixed operations, customer experience, analytics, and leadership teams free, inspectable playbooks for repeatable AI-assisted work

**October 2, 2026** — Dealer Growth Hackers today announced the public launch of [Dealer AI Plugins](https://dealeraiplugins.com/), a collection of 29 free, open-source plugins designed to help automotive dealerships use ChatGPT and Codex for specific operational tasks.

The launch follows Dealer Growth Hackers’ release of [DealerAISkills.com](https://dealeraiskills.com/), a companion collection of dealership workflows built for Claude. Dealer AI Plugins brings those proven workflows—and six additional coordination and governance plugins—to ChatGPT and Codex, giving dealership teams a platform-independent foundation for repeatable AI-assisted work.

Each plugin packages a focused dealership workflow into an inspectable set of instructions, references, checklists, templates, and examples. Instead of relying on a general AI assistant to invent a process from scratch, dealership teams can give it a defined playbook for jobs such as reviewing vehicle detail pages, auditing local search visibility, classifying sales calls, analyzing customer sentiment, planning email flows, evaluating lead response, and assessing AI-shopping readiness.

“Dealership teams do not need more vague promises about AI. They need practical workflows they can inspect, test, and adapt to the way their stores actually operate,” said Ariel Coro of Dealer Growth Hackers. “Dealer AI Plugins turns repeatable dealership knowledge into clear playbooks for ChatGPT and Codex while keeping people responsible for the final decision.”

The initial collection covers six major areas of dealership work:

- Sales and BDC
- Fixed operations
- Marketing
- Customer experience
- Analytics and technology
- Executive and dealership operations

The library also introduces three coordination workflows for more complex projects. **Muse** helps plan, sequence, and review multi-step work. **Dots** keeps tasks, dependencies, approvals, and handoffs visible. The **Dealer Plugin Router** helps select the smallest suitable specialist workflow for a request. A separate governance plugin helps teams define approval, evidence, privacy, and escalation boundaries.

Dealer AI Plugins is designed around human oversight. Installing a plugin does not automatically provide access to a dealership’s CRM, website, analytics, call-tracking account, or other private systems. Users decide what information or approved connections enter a conversation and review the resulting analysis before taking action.

Every public plugin is available at no charge, distributed under the MIT License, and can be inspected on GitHub. Dealer Growth Hackers offers optional implementation programs, enablement, and professional services separately; the free public plugins are not reduced versions of paid products.

The launch website includes:

- A searchable catalog of all 29 plugins
- Department and use-case filters
- Plain-language installation guidance
- Input-to-output diagrams for every workflow
- Explanations of Muse, Dots, and the Plugin Router
- Privacy, permission, and human-approval guidance
- Direct package downloads and source links

Dealer AI Plugins can be explored at [DealerAIPlugins.com](https://dealeraiplugins.com/). Source code and package documentation are available in the [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/arielcoro/dealer-ai-plugins).

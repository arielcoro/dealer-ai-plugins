# Dealer AI Ecosystem Strategy

Reviewed: October 1, 2026

## The organizing idea

Dealer Growth Hackers should be the visible publisher and operating center of the dealer-focused portfolio. The portfolio should behave like one customer journey with specialized destinations—not a ring of unrelated microsites and not a sitewide reciprocal-link network.

The shared journey is:

1. **Learn** — understand dealership AI and choose the right first use case.
2. **Measure** — establish a technical, visibility, or conversion baseline.
3. **Diagnose** — validate the evidence and create a prioritized work order.
4. **Build** — use open workflows or engage an implementation team.
5. **Connect** — expose governed live dealership facts to AI agents.
6. **Operate and grow** — deploy conversion, publishing, messaging, and commerce products.

## Linking rules

1. Every portfolio site links to one canonical **Dealer AI Ecosystem** directory.
2. Each site links contextually to only two to four immediate next steps in the customer journey.
3. Diagnostic sites use a consistent sequence: **baseline → human diagnosis → implementation → verification**.
4. Open knowledge sites cross-link by assistant: **Claude → Skills**, **ChatGPT/Codex → Plugins**, and general process work → **Workflows/Prompts**.
5. Infrastructure sites cross-link as a three-part system: **Protocol → MCP → Gateway**.
6. Product sites link to diagnostics that justify the product and to services that implement it.
7. Tu Tecnología and Coro Capital Partners remain related-company links on company/ecosystem pages only.
8. Avoid putting all portfolio domains in every footer. Large reciprocal footer blocks dilute relevance and confuse visitors.

# Active Dealer AI Plugin Catalog

26 installable packages. Package names are installation identities; skills inside a bundle do not require separate installation.

- **Website AI Readiness** (package `website-ai-readiness`): Assess website AI readiness with reproducible evidence, coverage-aware scores, and prioritized technical and content fixes.
- **Dealer AEO Content Brief** (package `dealer-aeo-content-brief`): Generate an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) content brief for a car dealership page or post.
- **Dealer AI Operations Readiness** (package `dealer-ai-readiness-audit`): Assess dealership operational AI readiness across systems, people, processes, data, and governance. This is an organizational adoption assessment, not a website or AI-shopping audit.
- **Dealer AI Referral Analytics** (package `dealer-ai-referral-analytics`): Audit a car dealership's AI-referrer traffic across GA4, Google Search Console, and server logs.
- **Dealer AI Visibility** (package `dealer-ai-visibility`): Measure dealership AI search visibility, citations, sentiment, factual accuracy, and competitor differences using a shared evidence log. Includes separate visibility and sentiment workflows; no guaranteed recommendations or traffic.
- **Dealer Bilingual SEO** (package `dealer-bilingual-seo`): Build, audit, or remediate a Spanish-language SEO presence for a US car dealership targeting Hispanic buyers.
- **Dealer Call Tracking Audit** (package `dealer-call-tracking-audit`): Audit a car dealership's call tracking platform configuration (CallRail, Invoca, Marchex) for attribution accuracy, conversation intelligence, and integration with GA4 and Google Ads.
- **Dealer Call Classifier** (package `dealer-call-classifier`): Classify car dealership call transcripts into five categories with strict honesty rules.
- **Dealer Comparison Builder** (package `dealer-comparison-page-builder`): Build one in-depth comparison page between a car dealership and a single named competitor for SEO, AEO, GEO, and sales enablement.
- **Dealer CTA Audit** (package `dealer-cta-audit`): Audit calls-to-action (CTAs) on car dealership website pages for conversion effectiveness.
- **Dealer Review Sentiment** (package `dealer-customer-sentiment`): Analyze car dealership customer reviews across multiple dimensions and surface honest patterns, themes, and named-staff mentions.
- **Dealer Email Flows** (package `dealer-email-flows`): Build comprehensive lifecycle email flows for a car dealership covering the full customer relationship from purchase through year 5+.
- **Dealer Equity Campaigns** (package `dealer-equity-mining`): Build a complete equity mining campaign for a car dealership.
- **Dealer GA4 Tracking Audit** (package `dealer-ga4-tracking-audit`): Audit a car dealership's GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking stack end-to-end.
- **Dealer GBP Audit** (package `dealer-gbp-audit`): Audit a car dealership's Google Business Profile (GBP) for local SEO and local pack performance.
- **Dealer llms.txt Generator** (package `dealer-llms-txt-generator`): Generate a curated llms.txt file for a car dealership website to improve AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and AI search visibility.
- **Dealer Customer Onboarding** (package `dealer-new-customer-onboarding`): Design a comprehensive 90-day post-sale customer onboarding program for a car dealership.
- **Dealer Search Strategy** (package `dealer-search-strategy`): Generate a comprehensive search strategy document for a car dealership covering SEO (Search Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).
- **Dealer SEO Audit** (package `dealer-seo-audit`): Run a comprehensive traditional Google SEO audit on a car dealership website.
- **Dealer Site Score** (package `dealer-site-score`): Run Dealer Site Score, the dealer website grader, on a franchise or independent car dealership site.
- **Dealer Store Positioning** (package `dealer-store-positioning`): Generate a comprehensive positioning and brand content kit for a car dealership.
- **Dealer VDP Review** (package `dealer-vdp-merchandising-review`): Review a sample of Vehicle Detail Pages (VDPs) on a car dealership website for merchandising quality.
- **Dealer Agent Governance** (package `dealer-agent-governance`): Define approval, evidence, privacy, and escalation rules for dealership assistants and agent workflows.
- **Dealer AI Workflow Coordinator** (package `dealer-plugin-router`): Coordinate dealership work with three included workflows: Router selects specialists, Dots guides interactive work, and Muse plans and reviews multi-workstream tasks. Preserves user control, explicit approval boundaries, and evidence-backed handoffs.
- **Dealer AI Shopping Readiness** (package `dealer-shopping-readiness`): Audit dealership website AI readiness, inventory accuracy, pricing clarity, business identity, reputation evidence, and shopper handoffs. Consolidates AEO and shopping-readiness checks into one evidence-backed assessment and prioritized fix plan.
- **Dealer Lead Response Audit** (package `dealer-lead-response-auditor`): Audit dealership lead-response speed, quality, cadence, appointment conversion, handoffs, consent, and measurement.

## Bundled skills

- dealer-ai-visibility includes visibility and dealer-ai-sentiment-monitor.
- dealer-plugin-router is Dealer AI Workflow Coordinator and includes Router, dots-assistant and muse-meta-assistant.
- dealer-shopping-readiness replaces dealer-aeo-audit; use website-ai-readiness for a general website-only assessment.
